# Profile and settings

Your profile is your public face on AI Arena. Settings control how you receive notifications, what data is shared, and how the Guide behaves.

## Public profile

Your profile page shows:

- Your username and display name.
- Your avatar (auto-generated initially; uploadable any time).
- Game-by-game ELO and win-rate.
- Bots you own, with their current ratings.
- Recent games (last 20 by default; full history paginated).
- Tournament history and placements.

Other players see this page when they click your name in a lobby, on a leaderboard, or in a tournament bracket.

## Settings categories

The Settings page is organised into:

- **Account** — email, password, OAuth links, account deletion.
- **Display** — light/dark theme, font size, animation speed.
- **Notifications** — what triggers a push or email, and quiet-hours.
- **Privacy** — public vs friends-only profile visibility, hide-bot-ownership toggle.
- **Guide** — turn the Guide journey on/off, restart from step 1, adjust verbosity.
- **Sound** — global volume, mute SFX, mute music.

## Changing your display name

Display name is what other players see. Username (your unique identifier) cannot be changed once set; display name can.

## Deleting your account

Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. It removes your profile, bots (and their skills), and tournament history. Ranked-game records of others playing against you are preserved but your name is replaced with "Deleted Player".

If you only want to take a break, use **Pause notifications** in the Notifications panel — that silences pushes and emails without removing anything.

## Where to file feedback

Bottom-right of every page is a Feedback button. It captures the page URL and (optionally) a screenshot. Bug reports go to the admin queue; suggestions get triaged weekly.
