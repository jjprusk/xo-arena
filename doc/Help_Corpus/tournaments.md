# Tournaments

Tournaments on AI Arena are scheduled or recurring multi-player events with a fixed bracket, a defined start time, and a prize in credits for top finishers.

## Types of tournaments

- **One-off tournaments** — created by an admin or tournament admin, run once, then end.
- **Recurring tournaments** — created from a template that fires on a schedule (daily, weekly, monthly). Anyone can subscribe to a recurring template; subscribers are auto-registered to each instance.
- **Cups** — special tournaments tied to the Guide curriculum (Curriculum Cup, Rookie Cup, etc.). These have curated brackets and unlock specific Guide steps.

## Formats

Bracket formats supported:

- **Single elimination** — lose once, you're out.
- **Double elimination** — lose twice, you're out. Winner's bracket + loser's bracket.
- **Round robin** — every participant plays every other.
- **Swiss** — paired by current standings each round; fixed number of rounds.

Each match is "best of N" — typically 1, 3, or 5 games — set when the tournament is created.

## Registering

For a one-off, register from the tournament's detail page. For a recurring tournament, click **Subscribe** on its template page to auto-register for every future instance. You can opt out any time from the My Tournaments page.

## Seeding

Brackets are seeded either **randomly** or **deterministically** (highest ELO at the top). Determined at template/tournament creation; visible on the bracket page so you know what to expect.

## What happens during the tournament

When a tournament starts, you get a notification with your first match. Your match is a **Table** with you, your opponent, and a best-of-N game. Click the notification to go to your table. If your opponent doesn't show up, the platform may auto-forfeit them after a timeout.

Between matches you're shown the bracket and your projected next opponent. When you win or lose your final match, your placement and prize (if any) are settled and credited.
