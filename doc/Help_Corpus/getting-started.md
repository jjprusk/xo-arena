# Welcome to AI Arena

AI Arena is a platform where you play classic games against AI bots, train your own bots, and compete in tournaments. Everything is designed to teach you about AI through play — no machine-learning background required.

## Sign up and sign in

You can create an account with **email + password** or sign in with **Google** (OAuth). Email verification is optional for most of the platform — you'll see a soft "Verify your email" banner until you confirm. Tournament entry requires a verified email.

There's no credit-card or paid plan. Every account starts with **zero credits** in all three currencies (TC, HPC, BPC) — you earn them by playing, completing journey steps, and unlocking discovery rewards. See the "Credits" doc for details.

## Your first game

The fastest way to start is the **Quick Play** button on the home page. It pairs you with a built-in bot picked to match your skill level. Your first game intentionally uses a weaker bot so you can get a feel for the interface.

If you're not signed in, the live bot-vs-bot demo on the home page lets you watch two bots play immediately. Signing up later transfers any guest progress you've made.

## The Intelligent Guide

When you sign in, the **Guide** appears on the right — an animated orb in the header that opens a slide-in drawer. The Guide walks you through a 7-step onboarding journey across three phases:

- **Hook** — Play one game; watch a bot-vs-bot demo. (Steps 1–2)
- **Curriculum** — Create your own bot; train it; spar; enter a tournament. (Steps 3–6)
- **Specialize** — Tournament complete. The Guide steps back; the platform opens up. (Step 7)

You can open or close the Guide any time with the orb. See the "Intelligent Guide" doc for what each step does and what it rewards.

## Where things live

The top navigation has six main destinations:

- **Tables** — open games waiting for opponents, plus your active tables.
- **Tournaments** — find and join scheduled, recurring, or flash tournaments.
- **Gym** — the bot training studio (8 tabs: Train, Analytics, Evaluation, Explainability, Checkpoints, Sessions, Export, Rules).
- **Rankings** — leaderboards by activity tier.
- **Bots** — browse and challenge bots, train your own.
- **Profile** — your stats, bots, tournament history, settings access.

A floating 💬 button in the bottom-right opens the Feedback panel — use it to report bugs or suggest features. The Guide drawer also has an "Ask Guide anything…" input.

## Settings, sign-out, and idle timeout

Open your avatar menu in the header for Settings and sign-out. Settings covers notification sounds, push notifications, event preferences, password, and account deletion.

If you leave the tab idle for 30 minutes (default), you'll get a "still there?" warning; another 5 minutes without activity signs you out automatically. The exact thresholds are admin-tunable.
