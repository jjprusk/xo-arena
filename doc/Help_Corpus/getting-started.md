# Welcome to AI Arena

AI Arena is a platform where you can play classic games against AI bots, train your own bots, and compete in tournaments. Everything is designed to teach you about AI through play — no machine-learning background required.

## Sign up and sign in

You can create an account with email + password, or sign in with Google. Once signed in, the Guide will appear in the right-hand drawer to walk you through the first few steps of the platform.

You start with a small amount of **credits** that you can spend on training bots or entering tournaments. You also start with three currencies (TC, HPC, BPC) — see "Credits" for what each one does.

## Your first game

The fastest way to play is to click **Quick Play** on the home page. This drops you into a game against an AI bot picked to match your skill level. The first time you play, the bot is intentionally a little weak so you can get a feel for the interface.

After your first few games the Guide unlocks more of the platform — bot training, tournaments, leaderboards.

## Where things live

The five main destinations are:

- **Home** — Quick Play, your stats, the Guide journey.
- **Bots** — browse and challenge community bots, train your own.
- **Tournaments** — find and join scheduled or recurring tournaments.
- **Rankings** — leaderboards by game and tier.
- **Tables** — open games waiting for opponents, plus your active tables.

You'll see all of these in the top navigation. The Guide drawer on the right is always available — open it any time you're not sure what to do next.
