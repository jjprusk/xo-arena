# Getting started with AI Arena — how do I get started?

**Short answer for new users:** sign up (top-right of any page), click **Quick Play** on the home page, then follow the Guide drawer's 7-step onboarding journey. Total time: about 30 minutes from "just landed here" to "first tournament entered". This is the first-time walkthrough for new users — your welcome / tutorial / quickstart, all in one.

If you're a new user asking "how do I get started", "where do I begin", "what should I do first", "how do I sign up", or "where's the tutorial", you're in the right place. This doc is the onboarding hub for AI Arena.

AI Arena (formerly known as XO Arena) is a competitive platform for classic games with trainable AI. You play AI bots, train your own, and enter tournaments — everything is designed to teach you about AI through play, with no machine-learning background required. It's free to use; accounts are free; play is free.

## The 3-step quickstart

The shortest path from "just landed here" to "playing a game" is three steps:

1. **Sign up** — top-right of any page. Email + password or Google (one click). It takes about 15 seconds. You don't need to verify your email to start playing — that's only required when you enter a tournament.
2. **Hit Quick Play** — the big button on the home page. You'll be paired against a built-in bot chosen to match your level. Your first game uses a deliberately weaker opponent so you can learn the interface.
3. **Follow the Guide drawer** — once signed in, the Guide opens on the right with a 7-step onboarding journey (play a game → watch a demo → train your first bot → spar → enter a tournament). Each step pays a small credit reward. The whole journey takes 20–30 minutes.

If you want to look around first, the live bot-vs-bot demo on the home page lets you watch two bots play without signing in.

## Creating an account ("how do I get an account?" / "how do I sign up?")

You can create an account two ways:

- **Email + password** — top-right of any page, click **Sign up**, enter email + password.
- **Google (OAuth)** — same place, click "Continue with Google."

Either flow logs you in immediately. Email verification is optional for most of the platform — you'll see a soft "Verify your email" banner until you confirm — but tournament entry requires a verified email.

There's no credit-card or paid plan. Every account starts with **zero credits** in all three currencies (TC, HPC, BPC) — you earn them by playing, completing journey steps, and unlocking discovery rewards. See the "Credits" doc for details.

## What can I do without registering (guest mode / no account)

You don't have to register or create an account to use parts of AI Arena. Without signing up or registering — i.e., as a guest — you can:

- **Watch the live bot-vs-bot demo** on the home page.
- **Browse the Bot Directory** to see what kinds of bots are out there.
- **Browse Tournaments** to see what's open and what the formats look like.
- **Spectate any public Table** — watch live games strangers are playing.

You DO need an account (signing up takes ~15s) to:

- **Play a game yourself** (Quick Play, Tables, Sparring).
- **Train a bot** (the Gym requires a signed-in user to attach bots to).
- **Enter a tournament** (you also need a verified email for this).
- **Use the Guide** ("Ask Guide anything…" — the Help input requires sign-in).

Signing up later transfers any guest play history into your account, so nothing is lost.

## Your first game

The fastest way to start is the **Quick Play** button on the home page. It pairs you with a built-in bot picked to match your skill level. Your first game intentionally uses a weaker bot so you can get a feel for the interface.

If you're not signed in, the live bot-vs-bot demo on the home page lets you watch two bots play immediately. Signing up later transfers any guest progress you've made.

## The Intelligent Guide

When you sign in, the **Guide** appears on the right — an animated orb in the header that opens a slide-in drawer. The Guide walks you through a 7-step onboarding journey across three phases:

- **Hook** — Play one game; watch a bot-vs-bot demo. (Steps 1–2)
- **Curriculum** — Create your own bot; train it; spar; enter a tournament. (Steps 3–6)
- **Specialize** — Tournament complete. The Guide steps back; the platform opens up. (Step 7)

You can open or close the Guide any time with the orb. See the "Intelligent Guide" doc for what each step does and what it rewards.

## Where things live

The top navigation has six main destinations:

- **Tables** — open games waiting for opponents, plus your active tables.
- **Tournaments** — find and join scheduled, recurring, or flash tournaments.
- **Gym** — the bot training studio (8 tabs: Train, Analytics, Evaluation, Explainability, Checkpoints, Sessions, Export, Rules).
- **Rankings** — leaderboards by activity tier.
- **Bots** — browse and challenge bots, train your own.
- **Profile** — your stats, bots, tournament history, settings access.

A floating 💬 button in the bottom-right opens the Feedback panel — use it to report bugs or suggest features. The Guide drawer also has an "Ask Guide anything…" input.

## Settings, sign-out, and idle timeout

Open your avatar menu in the header for Settings and sign-out. Settings covers notification sounds, push notifications, event preferences, password, and account deletion.

If you leave the tab idle for 30 minutes (default), you'll get a "still there?" warning; another 5 minutes without activity signs you out automatically. The exact thresholds are admin-tunable.
